Privacy & limits

How long files, links and receipts last

Files and previews
24 hours. Uploaded files, generated books, source manuscripts and previews become inaccessible at expiry. Scheduled cleanup removes the stored bytes. To delete a file sooner, open your Shelf and choose Delete.
Private download links
15 minutes. Anyone with the link can download the file until the link or file expires. Until the file expires, you can get a fresh link from your Shelf.
Delivery receipts
35 days. Palim then removes receipts during scheduled cleanup. Deleting your account removes them sooner.

Default file and account limits

File upload
20 MiB per EPUB or PDF.
Email attachment
About 18.2 MiB per file. Forward checks the size when you choose a file. The encoded email, including the attachment, must fit within 25 MiB.
Generated books
10 MiB per finished EPUB. Limits also apply to manuscript length, figure complexity and image dimensions.
Daily allowance
2 creation attempts, 6 uploads and 3 email submissions per account by default. Allowances reset at midnight UTC. Shared limits also apply across accounts. Sign-in codes have a smaller allowance and count towards the shared email limit.
Kindle addresses
5 saved addresses per account. Removing an address leaves previously submitted emails with the provider.

What Palim processes

Your inbox email identifies your Palim account. Better Auth manages short-lived email codes and browser sessions using this deployment’s private database. Palim does not request repository access. Your agent selects the manuscript or file it sends to the service. The hosted Worker and private storage process those bytes. There is no authoring model running inside Palim.

When you request delivery, Cloudflare Email receives the document and sends it to your registered Amazon document address. Amazon controls conversion, archiving and Kindle library access. Your agent platform and both providers have their own data practices.

Account data and deletion

To delete your account, open Settings and choose Delete account under Account. You can also ask the operator to remove your account. Your account settings stay saved until deletion.

Deleting your account immediately removes its records, active browser sessions, stored files and previews, saved Kindle addresses, personal MCP token and connected OAuth agent grants. Provider logs and books already emailed to Amazon follow those providers’ policies.

Browser sessions last seven days. Sign-in codes expire after five minutes and allow three attempts. Codes are encrypted in the database; expired codes and sessions are removed by scheduled cleanup. Session records include the connecting IP address and browser user agent. Short-lived authentication rate counters are removed after an hour of inactivity.

Palim keeps separate mail-attempt counters for 35 days to enforce sending limits. Application logs exclude manuscripts, raw destinations and provider error text.

Agent access

Connected applications
Access lasts up to 30 days. Review the permissions the app requests before you connect it. You can disconnect it at any time under Connected applications in Settings.
Personal MCP token
Until you replace or revoke it. This optional token grants read, create and send access. Palim stores only its SHA-256 digest.

Forwarded files

The forwarding path reads bounded metadata but does not rewrite, repair, recompress or watermark your EPUB or PDF. A hash verifies the submitted attachment. Amazon may convert the file it imports; Palim does not control that representation.

Approve your personal sender address in Amazon before your first send. Palim does not require a Kindle verification code. Amazon may apply its own checks.

An accepted email submission is not confirmed Kindle delivery. A timeout is not permission to send another copy. The service cannot read your Amazon library, replace old books or migrate annotations.

Operator responsibilities

Palim is independent open-source software by Tim Coy. Each self-hosted instance has its own operator and Cloudflare account.

Follow the deployment guide before opening a hosted instance to other people. Configure provider retention and disable Cloudflare email-preview storage, verify account limits and run the deployment and device checks. Keep private buckets private, and do not put credentials into the repository.

To report a security issue, follow the private reporting instructions. Use this service only for material you are entitled to send.
