TWO WAYS TO TURN THE PAGE

Something new.
Or something untouched.

01

Make a reading edition.

Ask your agent to explain a concept, walk through a repository or unpack a decision. Palim gives it chapters, diagrams, sources and space to breathe.

“Explain this architecture in a short illustrated guide. Send it to my Kindle.”
02

Send the original.

Already have an EPUB or PDF? Forward it without changing its content, cover or metadata. The attachment stays byte-for-byte identical.

“Send this ebook to my reader. Don’t change anything.”

THE FINE PRINT, IN PLAIN SIGHT

Your reading stays yours.

Private artifacts expire after 24 hours. The hosted service, Cloudflare and Amazon process documents you choose to send. Self-hosting is available.

Small by design.

Automatic delivery has a 5 MiB encoded-email limit. Bigger files remain downloadable. Original books are never silently compressed to make them fit.

Honest receipts.

Email submission is not proof of Kindle import. Palim never claims to read your library, migrate highlights or replace an old edition in place.
