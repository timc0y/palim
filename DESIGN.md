# Palim visual system

One machine. The landing is the mark, enlarged until it is the picture. The studio uses the same ink, paper and indigo. Manrope is only for reading text.

## Colour

Paper `#e8e5db`, ink `#1a1916`, indigo `#263fa9`. The landing deck and the studio bar are ink. Keys are paper. The one primary key is indigo.

## Type

IBM Plex Mono carries the wordmark, commands, labels and keys. Manrope carries the manuscript, the sample and the privacy page. Both are self-hosted. See `THIRD_PARTY.md`.

## Landing

No headline, tagline or feature list. The word, the fold, two install commands, a small copy key, and four keys: sample, studio, connect, source. Below 1080px the fold fits inside the viewport. Above that it bleeds off the right edge. A shelf of pixel books closes the page.

## Studio

One task at a time, on the paper. Task keys are the landing keys: a paper face, a four-pixel lip, indigo for the task in progress. The sign-in card is the one boxed surface. Forms keep visible labels. Optional setup stays in a disclosure. An error sits beside the control that failed.

## Reading

The sample is the same paper, ink and indigo. Its bar uses the mark. The generated cover is the ink side of that palette, with the fold and no extra label. Manrope stays the reading face.
